B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering

Four-year undergraduate program in computing and technology

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering is a comprehensive four-year program designed to provide students with a strong foundation in computer science principles, software development, and emerging technologies. Our curriculum combines theoretical knowledge with practical applications, preparing graduates for successful careers in the technology industry.

Program Highlights

Duration
4 Years (8 Semesters)
Intake
60 Students per year
Degree
Bachelor of Technology
Accreditation
UGC Approved

Curriculum Structure

Our curriculum is carefully designed to provide a progressive learning experience, starting with fundamental concepts and advancing to specialized areas of computer science and engineering.

First Year (Semesters 1 & 2)

  • CS101Programming Fundamentals with C
  • MA101Engineering Mathematics I
  • PH101Engineering Physics
  • CH101Engineering Chemistry
  • EG101Engineering Graphics
  • EN101English Communication
  • CS102Object-Oriented Programming
  • MA102Engineering Mathematics II

Career Opportunities

B.Tech Computer Science graduates have excellent career prospects in various sectors of the technology industry, from software development to emerging fields like artificial intelligence and data science.

Software Development
Design, develop, and maintain software applications using various programming languages and frameworks.
Companies: Microsoft, Google, Amazon, TCS, Infosys
Data Science & Analytics
Analyze large datasets to extract insights and build predictive models for business decision-making.
Companies: Netflix, Uber, Flipkart, IBM, Accenture
Artificial Intelligence
Develop AI systems, machine learning models, and intelligent applications for various industries.
Companies: OpenAI, NVIDIA, Tesla, Meta, DeepMind
Cybersecurity
Protect systems and networks from cyber threats through security analysis and implementation.
Companies: Cisco, Symantec, FireEye, CrowdStrike
Product Management
Lead product development cycles, from conception to launch, bridging technology and business.
Companies: Google, Facebook, Airbnb, LinkedIn
Entrepreneurship
Start your own technology company or join innovative startups to build cutting-edge solutions.
Opportunities: Tech Startups, Incubators, VC Firms

Admission Requirements

Eligibility Criteria

  • 12th standard with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics
  • Minimum 60% aggregate marks in qualifying examination
  • Valid scores in JEE Main or equivalent entrance exam
  • Age limit: 17-25 years at the time of admission

Application Process

  • Online application through university portal
  • Upload required documents and certificates
  • Pay application fee
  • Appear for counseling (if shortlisted)
