Library Services

Research Assistance
Professional librarians provide personalized research support, literature reviews, and database training sessions for students and faculty.
Digital Access Support
Technical support for accessing digital resources, VPN setup for off-campus access, and troubleshooting database connectivity issues.
Citation & Writing Help
Workshops on academic writing, citation styles (APA, IEEE, MLA), and proper research methodology for thesis and project work.
Study Spaces
Quiet study areas, group discussion rooms, and collaborative workspaces equipped with modern furniture and technology.
Mobile Library App
Mobile application for catalogue search, resource reservation, renewal requests, and real-time availability checking.
Extended Hours
24/7 digital access to online resources with extended physical library hours during examination periods and project deadlines.
