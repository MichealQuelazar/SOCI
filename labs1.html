World-Class Laboratory Infrastructure

The School of Computational Intelligence houses cutting-edge laboratory facilities equipped with the latest technology and software tools. Our labs provide students with hands-on experience in various domains of computer science and artificial intelligence.

Our Laboratory Facilities

Advanced Programming Laboratory
Equipped with high-performance workstations for software development, algorithm implementation, and competitive programming practice.

Key Features:

  • Latest IDEs and development environments
  • Multiple programming language support
  • Version control systems
  • Cloud development platforms
  • Code review and collaboration tools

Specifications:

Capacity: 60 Students
Workstations: Intel Core i7, 16GB RAM
Operating Systems: Windows, Linux, macOS

Lab Access & Guidelines

Access Hours

  • Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Saturday: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Sunday: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • 24/7 access for research students
  • Extended hours during project deadlines

Lab Guidelines

  • Valid student ID required for entry
  • No food or drinks allowed in labs
  • Report any equipment issues immediately
  • Follow software licensing agreements
  • Maintain clean and organized workspace
